# OTTO Founder Equity Framework

**CONFIDENTIAL — Not for distribution**

---

## 1. Context

OTTO is being built by a lead founder who has:
- Already developed the core product (working code)
- Turned down employment offers (Trimble) to pursue this
- Established customer relationships (Girteka, Waberer's, etc.)
- Deep domain expertise from building Nexogen's planning systems

Potential co-founders are former Nexogen colleagues with:
- Relevant domain expertise
- Industry credibility
- Complementary skills
- Existing professional relationship with lead founder

## 2. Equity Principles

### 2.1 What Equity Reflects

| Factor | Weight |
|--------|--------|
| Risk already taken | High |
| Risk being taken (salary, opportunity cost) | High |
| Unique contribution (skills, relationships) | Medium |
| Replaceability | Medium |
| Time of joining (earlier = more) | Medium |
| Future commitment | High |

### 2.2 Current Situation Assessment

| Factor | Lead Founder | Potential Partners |
|--------|--------------|-------------------|
| Built the product | ✓ | – |
| Turned down safe employment | ✓ | Unknown |
| Domain expertise | ✓ | ✓ |
| Customer relationships | ✓ | Partial |
| Taking salary risk going forward | ✓ | Unknown |
| Joining at idea stage | N/A | No (product exists) |

## 3. Recommended Equity Structure

## 4. Vesting

### 4.1 Standard Terms

**All founders vest, including lead founder.**

| Parameter | Standard Term |
|-----------|---------------|
| Vesting period | 4 years |
| Cliff | 1 year |
| Vesting schedule | Monthly after cliff |
| Cliff amount | 25% (1/4 of grant) |

### 4.2 Why Lead Founder Should Also Vest

- Shows good faith to partners
- Protects company if lead founder leaves/incapacitated
- Standard for institutional investors
- Aligns long-term incentives

### 4.3 Acceleration Provisions

| Trigger | Treatment |
|---------|-----------|
| Single trigger (acquisition) | Optional: 25-50% acceleration |
| Double trigger (acquisition + termination) | Recommended: 100% acceleration |
| Death/disability | 100% acceleration |
| Termination for cause | Forfeit unvested |
| Voluntary departure | Keep vested only |

## 5. Negotiation Framework

### 5.1 Opening Position

> "I've built OTTO, turned down Trimble, and have the customer relationships. I want you both in—your experience is valuable and we worked well together.
>
> I'm proposing 55/17.5/17.5 with a 10% option pool. Everyone vests over 4 years with a 1-year cliff. This gives me the lead position while giving you meaningful upside.
>
> What do you think?"

### 5.2 What's Negotiable

| Item | Flexibility |
|------|-------------|
| Exact percentages | ±3-5% |
| Vesting schedule | 3-4 years acceptable |
| Cliff duration | 6-12 months acceptable |
| Acceleration terms | Negotiable |
| Role definitions | Flexible based on skills |
| Salary levels | Based on funding/revenue |

### 5.3 What's Non-Negotiable

| Item | Rationale |
|------|-----------|
| Lead founder majority (>50%) | Control for the person who built it and leads it |
| Everyone vests | Alignment and protection |
| Option pool exists | Need flexibility for future hires |
| Lead founder is CEO | Unless explicitly choosing otherwise |

### 5.4 Red Flags

| Partner Behavior | Concern |
|------------------|---------|
| Demands equal split (33/33/33) | Doesn't recognize prior work and risk |
| Refuses to vest | Not committed long-term |
| Wants board control at minority stake | Disproportionate power grab |
| Wants guaranteed salary before revenue | Not aligned with startup risk |
| Negotiates aggressively before committing | May be difficult to work with |

### 5.5 Good Signs

| Partner Behavior | Indicates |
|------------------|-----------|
| Acknowledges your prior work | Reasonable, fair-minded |
| Willing to vest | Committed long-term |
| Asks about role, not just equity | Focused on contribution |
| Has ideas for what they'll do | Ready to add value |
| Willing to take salary risk | Aligned incentives |

## 6. Role Definitions

Equity should come with clear role expectations.

### 6.1 Example Role Split

| Role | Person | Responsibilities |
|------|--------|------------------|
| CEO | Lead Founder | Vision, strategy, fundraising, key customers |
| CTO | Partner A (if technical) | Architecture, engineering, product |
| COO/CRO | Partner B (if commercial) | Operations, sales, partnerships |

### 6.2 Decision Rights

| Decision Type | Who Decides |
|---------------|-------------|
| Day-to-day operations | Relevant role owner |
| Hiring (their team) | Role owner with CEO input |
| Product direction | CEO + CTO jointly |
| Major partnerships | CEO with board input |
| Fundraising terms | CEO with board approval |
| Sale of company | Board (requires majority/supermajority) |

## 7. Legal Structure

### 7.1 Documents Needed

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Founders' Agreement | Equity split, vesting, roles, IP assignment |
| Stock Purchase Agreements | Legal ownership of shares |
| IP Assignment | All prior work assigned to company |
| Vesting Agreement | Vesting schedule, acceleration, repurchase rights |
| Operating Agreement (if LLC) or Bylaws (if Corp) | Governance |

### 7.2 Entity Considerations

| Structure | Best For |
|-----------|----------|
| Delaware C-Corp | Planning to raise VC, potential acquisition by US company |
| EU equivalent (BV, GmbH, etc.) | Primarily EU operations, EU investors |
| LLC | Bootstrapping, pass-through taxation |

**Recommendation:** Delaware C-Corp if targeting US acquirers (Trimble, Samsara) or US VCs. Consider EU subsidiary for operations.

## 8. Scenario Planning

### 8.1 If a Partner Leaves Early

| Timing | Outcome |
|--------|---------|
| Before cliff (year 1) | They get nothing, shares return to pool |
| After cliff, before full vest | They keep vested portion (e.g., 40% at year 2.5) |
| After full vest | They keep everything |

### 8.2 If Company Is Acquired

| Timing | With Double Trigger Acceleration |
|--------|----------------------------------|
| Year 1 (pre-cliff) | Cliff accelerates, they get cliff amount |
| Year 2 | Unvested accelerates if terminated |
| Year 4+ | Fully vested, full payout |

### 8.3 If Lead Founder Leaves

This is the scenario partners will worry about. Address it:

> "I'm also vesting. If I leave before year 4, I don't keep everything. That protects you—if I disappear, the company isn't stuck with a majority owner who isn't contributing."

## 9. Compensation Beyond Equity

### 9.1 Salary Approach

| Phase | Salary Approach |
|-------|-----------------|
| Pre-revenue | Minimal/none, everyone takes risk |
| Early revenue (<€500k ARR) | Below-market, survival mode |
| Growth (€500k-2M ARR) | Market-ish, based on cash flow |
| Scale (>€2M ARR) | Market rate |

### 9.2 Founder Salary Benchmarks (EU)

| Role | Below-Market | Market |
|------|--------------|--------|
| CEO | €60-80k | €120-180k |
| CTO | €55-75k | €100-150k |
| COO | €50-70k | €90-130k |

**Principle:** Everyone takes the same salary cut percentage. If CEO takes 50% of market, CTO takes 50% of market.

## 10. Final Checklist

Before finalizing equity:

- [ ] All founders understand and accept the split
- [ ] Vesting terms are agreed and documented
- [ ] Roles and responsibilities are clear
- [ ] Decision rights are defined
- [ ] Salary expectations are aligned
- [ ] IP assignment is signed
- [ ] Legal entity is established
- [ ] Founders' agreement is signed
- [ ] Everyone has independent legal review (recommended)
